# Weekly professor meeting

The format and the professor's requests, verbatim. Apply them every time the owner asks to
prepare the weekly professor meeting. (The team meeting has no fixed format; see SKILL.md
principle 11.)

## Format (verbatim)

Each of the four is **one slide**; detail slides (appendix) follow. The meeting is about
**15-20 minutes, discussion included**, so the four main slides get about 10 minutes of talk.

```
Claim and Validation Plan
  Claim (What The Study Showing)
  하이레벨클레임보다는 일주일에 검증할 수 있는 단위로 쪼개 그 한두개 단위에 대해 논의
  Validation Plan
This Week's Results in Single Line
TBD in Next Week
Where I've Been Stuck
```

## The professor's requests (verbatim)

- 실험/구현등 1주일간의 실질적 진행을 포함해주세요. (ideation의 경우도 "이런이런 주제를 해볼수
  있다" 보다는 다음에 바뀌더라도 "구체적 아이디어를 최대한 진전"시켜 와주세요)
- 다음 1주일의 계획을 명확히 밝혀주세요. (향후 할일 나열 x, priority와 시간을 고려하여 향후 1주일간
  먼저 해볼일 (o))
- 고민 사항/막히는 부분을 포함해주세요.
- AI가 생성하고 본인이 이해/검증하지 않은 내용은 미팅 자료에서 제외해주세요.
- 연구진행이 없는 주간이 가급적 발생하지 않도록 해주세요.

## What each slide must do

- **Claim and Validation Plan:** a claim that could be rejected, its hierarchy and the status of
  each part (`claim-slide.md`), and two or three ordered validation steps whose first step fits
  in the coming week. Background slides before it stay few.
- **This Week's Results in Single Line:** real progress, specific enough that someone could
  disagree with it. Framing progress counts when its concrete content is stated (what is
  exploited, where the opportunity comes from); a generic sentence does not.
- **TBD in Next Week:** one to three items in priority order, each with days and a done
  criterion, clearly different from each other, one line each.
- **Where I've Been Stuck:** one real question that blocks the first task of next week; no list
  made up to fill the slide.
- **Appendix:** supporting material shown only when a question calls for it.
